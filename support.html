Help & beta guide

A little help
with your whispers.

Your first memory

  1. Open Create and choose or take a photo.
  2. Write a short note. Add a title, tags, or a passphrase if you want.
  3. Review and save. Your memory appears in Vault → Whispers.

Common questions

What can I scan?

A photo or scene already saved in WhisperPic. Open Scan, tap Start scanning, match the saved angle, and hold steady. Try a well-lit, detailed reference first. A recipient must import the photo and note before scanning. Matching is experimental in this beta and can miss a scene after changes in angle, lighting, or layout.

How do I share a picture with its note?

In app versions with Share a whisper, choose Create & share link from a saved memory. This uploads only that photo and note as an encrypted copy for seven days. Recipients open the complete link in their browser and tap to reveal, without installing an app or creating an account. A protected note still needs its passphrase, sent separately. You can share an existing link again, preview it, or turn it off. Settings → Shared links also manages links after local memory deletion.

For offline file exchange, open File transfer & backup and use Share photo. This exports a normal viewable PNG with embedded note data. Send the original without compression, preferably as a file/document. The recipient imports it into WhisperPic. A .wsp backup also needs WhisperPic. Messaging apps that resize, compress, convert or edit the image can remove the note. A regular photo viewer shows the picture, not the hidden note.

How do I enable my photo backdrop?

Tap Connect photos on Scan or Create, or open Vault → Settings → Photo backdrop. Choose which photos the app can access. Expo Go and the standalone app have separate preferences, so enable it again after installing the store app. The background is visible on the main screens and paused Scan; the active camera uses a plain background. Background photos stay on your device. Cloud-only originals are skipped until available locally.

Why are some library pictures missing?

Your phone may allow access only to selected photos. Use the photo-access controls in Vault or device settings to select more or allow full photo access. Cloud-only pictures may need to be downloaded in your phone's Photos app before use. Browsing the library does not automatically add notes.

Does it work without the internet?

Creating, reading, and matching memories already available on your device work offline. Creating links, opening a link for the first time, cloud photo downloads, external sharing services, app installation, and these support pages require a connection.

I forgot a passphrase

WhisperPic does not store passphrases and has no recovery service. A locked note needs its original passphrase. The visible photo remains available.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a memory in its detail view to remove it from the vault. To remove all local app data, delete the app on iOS or clear app storage/uninstall on Android. Original library photos and shared/exported copies are separate. Read the deletion guide.

Contact & beta feedback

For a private beta report, use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight or the app's private feedback option on Google Play. Include your phone model, OS, WhisperPic version, steps to reproduce, and what you expected.

For general support or a privacy inquiry, email dpolokhalo@gmail.com. Include only the information needed to explain the issue; never send passphrases. You may also open a GitHub support request. GitHub requests are public; never post private photos, notes, passphrases, or personal identifiers.

WhisperPic does not include recipient notifications, account sync, voice notes, or self-contained message recovery from an unfamiliar print. Optional links require a version with link-sharing controls. Export important memories before uninstalling or changing devices.
