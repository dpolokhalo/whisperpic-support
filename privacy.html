Privacy policy

WhisperPic works with your photos and notes on your device. We do not operate an app account system or a server that receives your photos or notes.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the WhisperPic native iOS and Android app, maintained through the GitHub account @dpolokhalo. For privacy questions, use the support contact mechanism. Separate prototypes or third-party services have their own policies.

Photos, notes, and camera access

The system photo picker lets you select a photo for a memory. If you choose to browse your photo library in Vault or enable the optional photo backdrop, WhisperPic requests photo access and respects the full or selected-photo access you grant. Library dates and photo references are used to organize the view. Library browsing does not automatically create memories.

Camera permission is used to take a reference picture or match a live view against references saved in WhisperPic. Frames and visual matching features are processed locally. WhisperPic does not use camera matching to identify people, build facial identities, or send camera frames to us. The app does not request microphone, location, contacts, or advertising-tracking access.

What stays on the device

Saved memories include an app-owned photo copy, the attached note, optional title and tags, dates, and visual matching features. The app also stores preferences such as language and backdrop opt-in. These are held in the app's files and local database. The app has no analytics or remote crash-reporting SDK configured and does not send this content to us.

Passphrases and visible photos

A passphrase-protected note is encrypted using AES-256-GCM with a PBKDF2-SHA-256 derived key. Passphrases are not intentionally persisted by WhisperPic. The picture remains visible. Notes without a passphrase are not encrypted, and anyone with the file and a compatible decoder can read them. Hidden content alone is not access control. We cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

Sharing, imports, and backups

Sharing is initiated by you through the operating system's share sheet. The chosen recipient or service receives the photo and embedded note or exchange file, including encrypted content when protected. Send passphrases separately. Your chosen services and file providers handle data under their own policies. WhisperPic does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

WhisperPic provides no account-based cloud sync. Android automatic app backup is disabled, and the app does not enable iCloud document storage. Operating-system device backups, iCloud Photos downloads, exported files, and third-party sharing or backup services may still create copies depending on your settings. A cloud-only source photo may need to be downloaded by your photo provider before it can be used offline.

Retention and deletion

Saved memories are retained locally until you delete them or remove the app's data. Deleting a memory removes it from the vault and attempts to remove its app-owned photo; this does not delete the original library photo. Temporary import, camera, or sharing copies may remain in the app cache until cleaned up or the app's data is removed. Disable the optional backdrop to clear its active references. Revoke photo or camera access in device settings at any time.

To remove the complete local vault and cache, delete the app on iOS (not just offload it), or clear its storage/uninstall it on Android. Export anything you want to keep first. Delete exported files, backups, and shared copies separately. We do not hold a server copy that can be remotely deleted. See your data and deletion.

Support and this website

If you submit a support request, you choose what information to provide. GitHub issues are public and require a GitHub account; avoid posting private photos, notes, passphrases, personal details, or unredacted logs. Private beta feedback can be sent through TestFlight or Google Play. Support records are retained by the relevant service until removed according to its controls and policy.

This static support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no advertising, analytics scripts, embedded trackers, or forms that upload photos. GitHub may process standard web request information such as your IP address and user agent under the GitHub privacy statement. Opening web support links requires internet access; the app's core photo and note features do not.

Changes and questions

Changes will be published here with an updated date. For questions or privacy requests, use Support; do not include sensitive material in a public issue.
