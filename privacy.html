Privacy policy

WhisperPic keeps your photo library and vault on your device, with no app accounts or tracking. In versions offering Share a whisper, you can explicitly create an encrypted online copy of one selected photo and its note so a recipient can open a link in a browser.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the WhisperPic native iOS and Android app, maintained through the GitHub account @dpolokhalo. For privacy questions, email dpolokhalo@gmail.com or use Support. Separate prototypes or third-party services have their own policies.

Photos, notes, and camera access

The system photo picker lets you select a photo for a memory. If you choose to browse your photo library in Vault or enable the optional photo backdrop, WhisperPic requests photo access and respects the full or selected-photo access you grant. Library dates and photo references are used to organize the view. Library browsing does not automatically create memories.

Camera permission is used to take a reference picture or match a live view against references saved in WhisperPic. Frames and visual matching features are processed locally. WhisperPic does not use camera matching to identify people, build facial identities, or send camera frames to us. The app does not request microphone, location, contacts, or advertising-tracking access.

What stays on the device

Saved memories include an app-owned photo copy, the attached note, optional title and tags, dates, and visual matching features. The app also stores preferences such as language and backdrop opt-in, plus local counts of completed memory saves and the times/versions of review-prompt attempts. These review counters contain no photo, note, or passphrase and are not sent to us. These are held in the app's files and local database. The app has no analytics or remote crash-reporting SDK configured. Browsing your library, using a photo backdrop, and saving a memory do not upload those photos or notes.

One-time purchases

Versions offering WhisperPic Unlimited include three free new creations, followed by an optional one-time purchase for unlimited creation. Your saved whispers, scanning, importing and file sharing remain available without buying. The free-use counter is local to this installation and is not reset when you delete a memory. No WhisperPic account is created.

Apple or Google processes checkout and payment details under its own policies; WhisperPic does not receive your card details. We keep the verified product, store, transaction reference and verification time in secure device storage to allow offline creation. Restore purchase uses the same store account; Apple and Google purchases are separate.

On Android, purchase or restore verification sends the Google Play purchase token and fixed product identifier to the existing WhisperPic receiver, which checks them with the Google Play Developer API. No photo, note or passphrase is included. Our verification code does not retain raw purchase tokens or Google account details. It returns a hashed transaction reference for the device cache. Hourly hashed IP counters limit verification abuse and expire after an hour. Hosting and network providers may process request metadata as described below. Initial purchase and restore require an internet connection; a previously verified unlock is available offline. Store refunds or revocations may remove the unlock when ownership is checked again.

Passphrases and visible photos

A passphrase-protected note is encrypted using AES-256-GCM with a PBKDF2-SHA-256 derived key. Passphrases are not intentionally persisted by WhisperPic. The picture remains visible. Notes without a passphrase are not encrypted, and anyone with the file and a compatible decoder can read them. Hidden content alone is not access control. We cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

Optional encrypted whisper links

Link sharing is separate from offline PNG and .wsp file exchange. It is available only in app versions that show the link-sharing controls. Tapping Create & share link encrypts the chosen photo preview and its existing note packet on your device using a fresh AES-256-GCM key, then uploads the ciphertext to the WhisperPic browser receiver hosted on ChatGPT Sites infrastructure. Nothing else in your library or vault is uploaded. Creating a link and first opening it require internet access.

The decryption key is placed after the # in the complete link. It is not sent with the ciphertext upload or ordinary web request to the receiver. Anyone with the complete link can obtain its photo and an unprotected note; a passphrase-protected note still needs its separately shared passphrase. Share links and passphrases carefully. We cannot recover forgotten note passphrases.

The service stores encrypted content, random share IDs, creation/expiry times, hashes of revocation and short-lived upload capabilities, and bounded abuse-prevention counters. Application code uses a daily one-way hash of a source IP address for upload limits, not a stored raw IP address. Hosting/network providers may process IP addresses, requests and operational logs under their own policies. WhisperPic does not use link content for advertising or analytics.

Links stop serving seven days after creation, or after you turn them off from the saved memory or Settings → Shared links. Already downloaded or saved copies cannot be recalled. Expired content is made inaccessible at its deadline; physical cleanup of expired ciphertext is opportunistic as subsequent shares are created and is not guaranteed at exactly seven days. Local deletion does not turn off existing links. Link-management capabilities are stored only in the sender's app data; uninstalling or clearing that data removes the ability to revoke them from that installation, while expiry still applies.

Sharing, imports, and backups

Sharing is initiated by you through the operating system's share sheet. The chosen recipient or service receives the photo and embedded note or exchange file, including encrypted content when protected. Send passphrases separately. Your chosen services and file providers handle data under their own policies. WhisperPic does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

WhisperPic provides no account-based cloud sync. Android automatic app backup is disabled, and the app does not enable iCloud document storage. Operating-system device backups, iCloud Photos downloads, exported files, and third-party sharing or backup services may still create copies depending on your settings. A cloud-only source photo may need to be downloaded by your photo provider before it can be used offline.

App ratings and reviews

WhisperPic may request a rating through Apple or Google after you have used the app. The store controls whether its optional review card appears. If you submit feedback, Apple or Google processes it under your store account and its policies. Public reviews can include your store profile and chosen review text; closed-test feedback may be shared privately with the developer. Review text is not added to your photo or vault. Manage or delete store reviews through your Apple or Google account.

Retention and deletion

Saved memories are retained locally until you delete them or remove the app's data. Deleting a memory removes it from the vault and attempts to remove its app-owned photo; this does not delete the original library photo. Temporary import, camera, or sharing copies may remain in the app cache until cleaned up or the app's data is removed. Disable the optional backdrop to clear its active references. Revoke photo or camera access in device settings at any time.

To remove the complete local vault and cache, delete the app on iOS (not just offload it), or clear its storage/uninstall it on Android. Export anything you want to keep first. Delete exported files, backups, and shared copies separately. For optional encrypted links, turn off active links in Settings → Shared links before removing app data, or let them expire. See your data and deletion.

Support and this website

If you submit a support request, you choose what information to provide. GitHub issues are public and require a GitHub account; avoid posting private photos, notes, passphrases, personal details, or unredacted logs. Private beta feedback can be sent through TestFlight or Google Play. Email support is handled through the support mailbox and its email provider. Support records are retained for responding to the request and follow-up, or until removed according to the relevant service’s controls and policy.

This static support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no advertising, analytics scripts, embedded trackers, or forms that upload photos. GitHub may process standard web request information such as your IP address and user agent under the GitHub privacy statement. Opening web support links requires internet access; the app's core photo and note features do not.

Changes and questions

Changes will be published here with an updated date. For questions or privacy requests, use Support; do not include sensitive material in a public issue.
