Help & beta guide

To reveal a shared note, the recipient imports the original PNG or WhisperPic exchange file into WhisperPic. Editing, compression, screenshots, and printing can remove embedded note data. Live scanning matches previously saved references; it does not discover notes in unfamiliar pictures.
