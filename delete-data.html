WhisperPic has no app accounts. Your vault stays on your device; an optional sharing link creates a separate encrypted online copy of only the selected photo and note.

Request deletion of an online whisper

In WhisperPic, open Settings → Shared links and turn off the link. This requests removal of its encrypted online copy and link record. Do this before removing app data. Without the local revocation capability, we cannot confirm ownership from a photo alone. For help, email dpolokhalo@gmail.com with “WhisperPic data deletion.” Do not send your photo, note, passphrase, purchase token or complete secret link.

Links stop serving after seven days if not revoked; expired ciphertext cleanup is opportunistic. Daily sharing-abuse counters and hourly verification-abuse counters expire after their quota window and are removed during subsequent service requests. Those counters cannot be located by your name because there is no app account. Hosting providers may retain operational metadata under their policies. A recipient’s downloaded or forwarded copy is independent.

Delete one memory

Open Vault → Whispers, open the memory, choose Delete, and confirm. This removes it from the app vault and attempts to delete its app-owned picture. It does not delete the original photo in your phone's library.

Remove all local app data

Export any memories you want to keep first. On iOS, choose Delete App rather than Offload App. On Android, use the app's storage settings to Clear storage / Clear data, or uninstall it. This removes the local app database, preferences, and cache from the active installation. Operating-system backups may restore data later if you choose to restore them.

Purchase records

Removing app data does not erase your purchase history with Apple or Google. On iOS, the cached unlock may remain in the system Keychain after app deletion; it contains the product, store, transaction reference and verification time, not your photos or notes. Restore purchase checks ownership with the same store account. Manage purchase records and refunds through Apple or Google.

Remove other copies

Delete exported PNG and .wsp files, cloud or device backups, and copies in your chosen sharing services separately. Ask recipients to delete their copies if needed; WhisperPic cannot recall files already shared. Revoke photo/camera access in device settings or switch off the optional backdrop in WhisperPic settings.

To stop future opens of an encrypted sharing link, use Settings → Shared links before deleting app data. Local memory deletion does not revoke links, and copies already received remain independent. Links otherwise stop serving after seven days; expired ciphertext cleanup is opportunistic. There is no WhisperPic account to close. For a question about data handling, use Support. See the privacy policy for retention details.
